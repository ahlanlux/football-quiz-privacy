Privacy Policy

Football Guess Quiz (Android app com.aziz.football_quiz) · Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what data Football Guess Quiz ("the app") uses. The app is made by Aziz Marzouki, an independent developer ("I", "me"). In short: I do not collect or store your personal data. The only third-party service is Google AdMob, which shows optional rewarded videos.

1. Data I collect

None. The app has no account, no sign-in, and no analytics, and it does not send your data to me.

2. Data stored only on your device

This data stays in the app's private storage on your phone. I cannot see it. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows rewarded video ads only when you tap to watch one (for example, to get tokens or coins). There are no banner or pop-up ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)
  • Ad interactions (ads shown, watched, tapped)

Google processes this data under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

4. Your choices and consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form (Google User Messaging Platform) before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the app: About → Privacy options.
  • Everyone: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Watching ads is always optional. The game can be played without them.

The app is not directed to children under 13 and is not part of Google Play's Families program. I do not knowingly collect data from children.

Football facts come from Wikidata and Wikipedia. Photos come from Wikimedia Commons under free licences, listed in the app (About → Photo credits). Club crests are trademarks of their owners and are shown only to identify the clubs. The app is not affiliated with any club, league, FIFA or UEFA.

8. Data security and retention

I hold no personal data, so there is nothing for me to store, share or sell. Data on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Google keeps advertising data according to its own policies.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data. Because I hold no personal data, these requests usually concern Google's ad data: use the controls in section 4 or contact Google. You can also contact me with any question.
